North Bay → Algonquin

Hwy 11 S | ~2 hrs | ~185 km | 4 stops
Heading south through the Mattawa corridor and into the heart of Ontario cottage country, ending at the eastern gateway to Algonquin Park.

Points of Interest

I12-01
Voyageur Heritage Centre (Mattawa)
At the Mattawa/Ottawa River junction; key fur-trade meeting point. Champlain passed through in 1615.
ADDR 160 Water St, Mattawa, ON P0H 1V0, Canada
GPS 46.3191213, -78.7060780

Hikes

H12-01
Ragged Falls (Oxtongue River–Ragged Falls PP)
Short trail off Hwy 60 near Dwight to a waterfall ranked among Ontario's top ten; protected within its own provincial park.
ADDR 1050 Oxtongue Lake Rd, Dwight, ON P0A 1H0, Canada
GPS 45.3906707, -78.9079829

Places to Stay

P12-01
Algonquin Provincial Park (Hwy 60 Corridor)
Ontario's oldest, most iconic park (est. 1893). 56km corridor: moose, wolf howls, 8 campgrounds, dozens of trails.
ADDR Highway 60 at km 43, Algonquin Provincial Park, ON K0J 2M0, Canada
GPS 45.5839494, -78.3593196
P12-02
Arrowhead Provincial Park
Just north of Huntsville, Hwy 11/60 junction. Excellent hiking, swimming hole, fall colours.
ADDR 451 Arrowhead Park Rd, Huntsville, ON P1H 2J4, Canada
GPS 45.3915527, -79.2146783
