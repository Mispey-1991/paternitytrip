Northern Ontario Paternity Trip · Segment 12 of 13

North Bay → Algonquin Park

Hwy 11 S then Hwy 60 W | ~2 hrs | ~185 km | 4 stops
The final overnight stop before home. The Hwy 60 corridor through Algonquin is one of Ontario's great drives — wildlife-rich, stunning in all seasons, and lined with lodges and outfitters.
🦌 🦌 The Hwy 60 corridor through Algonquin is prime moose habitat — highest risk at dawn and dusk, when they come to the roadside.
⚠️ ⛽ Services inside the park are limited. Fill in Huntsville or North Bay before entering the corridor.

At the Mattawa/Ottawa River junction; key fur-trade meeting point. Champlain passed through in 1615.

Algonquin Provincial Park (Hwy 60 Corridor)
