Northern Ontario Paternity Trip · Segment 10 of 13

Timmins → New Liskeard

Hwy 11 S | ~2 hrs | ~185 km | 9 stops
The drive south through the Clay Belt toward Lake Timiskaming. The Silver Belt of Cobalt — one of the richest silver strikes in world history — is the highlight of this segment.

Points of Interest

I10-01
Cobalt Mining Museum
1903: one of the world's richest silver deposits discovered here. Museum is exceptional.
ADDR 24 Silver St, Cobalt, ON P0J 1C0, Canada
GPS 47.3955942, -79.6859297
I10-02
Devil's Rock (Temiskaming Shores)
Cliff rising 100m above fjord-like Lake Timiskaming; outstanding viewpoint.
ADDR Unorganized West Timiskaming District, ON P0J 1R0, Canada
GPS 47.4032433, -79.5904876
I10-03
Matheson Great Fire of 1916 Site
One of the deadliest Canadian wildfires, July 29 1916; killed at least 223 people in an afternoon.
ADDR Black River-Matheson, ON, Canada (fire area, no single fixed site)
GPS 48.5329, -80.4683

Hikes

H10-01
Upland Circle Trail (Kap-Kig-Iwan)
Easiest of the park's 3 trails: 2.5 km loop, ~1 hr, clifftop woods above the Englehart River. Gentlest option with an infant in tow.
ADDR Kap-Kig-Iwan Rd, Englehart, ON P0J 1H0, Canada
GPS 47.8007, -79.8803
Researched by Krista
H10-02
Hell's Gate Trail (Kap-Kig-Iwan)
The park's signature hike: 5 km, ~2.5 hrs, joins the Upland Circle Trail plus a riverside stretch past rapids and the main falls. Sheltered picnic pavilions along the way.
ADDR Kap-Kig-Iwan Rd, Englehart, ON P0J 1H0, Canada
GPS 47.8007, -79.8803
Researched by Krista
H10-03
Englehart Riverside Hiking Trail (Kap-Kig-Iwan)
Most demanding of the 3 trails: 5.7 km, ~3.5 hrs, rocky hilltops, bridges, muddy sections. Good hiking shoes needed, not stroller/carrier-friendly.
ADDR Kap-Kig-Iwan Rd, Englehart, ON P0J 1H0, Canada
GPS 47.8007, -79.8803
Researched by Krista
