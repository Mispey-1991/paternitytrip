Northern Ontario Paternity Trip · Segment 1b of 13

Tobermory → Little Current

MS Chi-Cheemaun ferry + Hwy 6 N | ~70 km | ~1 hr | 12 stops

The ferry crossing itself is the centerpiece of this segment. The MS Chi-Cheemaun (Anishinaabemowin for "Great Spirit"), a 130-meter icebreaker, is the sole road link to Manitoulin Island. The route continues up the east shore of Manitoulin to Little Current, the island's gateway port, over about 70 km and 1 hour of driving (ferry time not included). The crossing opens the island's interior and western shore to the final three Algonquin-approach segments.

Ferry check-in closes 1 hour before sailing. The Chi-Cheemaun runs on a fixed schedule; arriving late means missing your slot and a potentially hours-long delay. This is the single most time-critical constraint in the entire trip.
Propane and fuel declarations are mandatory. Bring your vehicle's propane or fuel vendor documentation with you; the ferry will not allow undeclared propane or fuel aboard.
Return to car prohibited underway. Once the ferry departs, you are not permitted to return to your vehicle until South Baymouth. If you're traveling with an infant or a dog, this is a major quality-of-life constraint.
