Northern Ontario Paternity Trip · Segment 7 of 13

Geraldton → Hearst

Hwy 11 E | ~3 hrs | ~275 km | 8 stops
One of the most remote stretches of the entire trip. The highway was completed in 1943 as a wartime emergency — the final link that first connected Canada by road from coast to coast.
⛽ ⛽ CRITICAL — Longlac is the last fuel eastbound. The next station is Hearst, 208 km away, with no settlements of any kind in between. Fill in Longlac, not Geraldton.
📵 📵 Expect no reliable cell coverage between Longlac and Hearst. (Verify before departure — inferred from the corridor's remoteness, not yet confirmed on the ground.)
🦌 🦌 Hearst bills itself the Moose Capital of Canada. Moose are a frequent and dangerous presence along this stretch, especially at dawn and dusk.
⚠️ 🏥 Nearest emergency care is Geraldton, 40 km west of Longlac, or Hearst at the far end. Nothing in between.
⚠️ ⚠️ Northern stations can close early — fill before dark. Check Ontario 511; closures here often have no detour.
