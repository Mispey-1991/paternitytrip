Northern Ontario Paternity Trip · Segment 9 of 13

Cochrane → Timmins

Hwy 101 W | ~1.5 hrs | ~130 km | 5 stops
A short segment between two of the most historically significant towns in Northern Ontario. Timmins was one of the world's richest gold mining centres.

Points of Interest

I9-01
Anson General Hospital Museum
In Iroquois Falls, a pulp/paper company town on the Abitibi River. Built 1921.
ADDR 58 Anson Dr, Iroquois Falls, ON P0K 1E0, Canada
GPS 48.7688514, -80.6853945
I9-02
Porcupine Gold Rush History
Timmins' 1909 gold rush eventually produced 67M+ ounces. Surface and underground mine tours available.
ADDR Hollinger Open Pit Lookout, Timmins, ON P4N 7N5, Canada
GPS 48.4716434, -81.3222374
I9-03
Shania Twain Centre
City-owned museum celebrating Timmins native Shania Twain's life and career. Operated 2001–2013; the building has since been demolished and the site repurposed. Worth a drive-by on Algonquin Blvd E, but no longer a functioning attraction.
ADDR 220 Algonquin Blvd E, Timmins, ON (closed 2013; site repurposed)
GPS 48.4697, -81.3167
