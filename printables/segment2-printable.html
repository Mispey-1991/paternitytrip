Northern Ontario Paternity Trip · Segment 2 of 13

Little Current → Sault Ste. Marie

Hwy 6 N then Hwy 17 W | ~3.25 hrs | ~300 km | 8 stops
From Little Current, cross the swing bridge off Manitoulin and pick up Hwy 6 north to Espanola, then turn west on Hwy 17 along the North Channel of Lake Huron toward the Sault, gateway to Lake Superior and home to the famous Algoma Central Railway.
⚠️ ⌂ Little Current swing bridge — the only road link off Manitoulin. In summer it opens for boat traffic during the first 15 minutes of each daylight hour; aim to reach it in the second half of the hour, not the first.
