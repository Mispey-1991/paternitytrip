Northern Ontario Paternity Trip · Segment 3 of 13

Sault Ste. Marie → Wawa

Hwy 17 N | ~2.5 hrs | ~235 km | 10 stops
One of the most dramatic drives in Ontario — the highway clings to the rugged north shore of Lake Superior through Lake Superior Provincial Park.
📵 📵 No cell coverage between Sault Ste. Marie and Wawa. Hilly terrain degrades radio reception too.
⛽ ⛽ Fill in Sault Ste. Marie. Last dependable fuel is Batchawana Bay (~75 km north); Pancake Bay is seasonal, and Montreal River Harbour is reported to close unpredictably — do not count on it. Expect roughly 2 hours between stations.
⚠️ ⚠️ Montreal River Hill is a long, steep grade — take it slow with a loaded vehicle.
