Northern Ontario Paternity Trip · Segment 11 of 13

New Liskeard → North Bay

Hwy 11 S | ~2 hrs | ~175 km | 7 stops
Heading south through the beautiful Temagami wilderness — one of Ontario's great canoe country regions — before arriving at North Bay, the 'Gateway to the North'.
🦌 Moose are common through the Temagami stretch of Hwy 11 at dawn and dusk.
