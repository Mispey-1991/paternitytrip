Hikes

H11-01
Caribou Mountain Fire Tower (Temagami)
Restored 1951 fire tower on the summit of Caribou Mountain — highest point on Hwy 11. 360° views up to 40+ km; overlooks Lake Temagami and old-growth white pine forest. Access: Off Hwy 11, take Jack Guppy Way to the parking lot near the summit — a 15-min drive from town. Trail from lot to tower base is 115m, gravel, moderate uphill. Total time ~1 hr. Tower climb: Regular stairs, then spiral staircases, then a short ladder to the cupola. The ladder section may be awkward with a baby carrier — the viewing platforms below it still give nearly identical views. $3 cash fee to climb. Open May–Thanksgiving. Dog: Welcome on leash on trails and at the base; the climb itself will depend on comfort with the ladder. White Bear Trail (old-growth pine loop) also accessible from the same parking lot.
ADDR 112 Jack Guppy Way, Temagami, ON P0H 2H0, Canada
GPS 47.0582249, -79.7793275
