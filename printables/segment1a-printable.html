Northern Ontario Paternity Trip · Segment 1a of 13

Whitby → Tobermory

Hwy 10 N / Hwy 6 N via Owen Sound | ~4 hrs | ~310 km | 15 stops
The trip opens to the northwest rather than due north. Hwy 10 N and Hwy 6 N run up the narrowing limestone spine of the Bruce Peninsula, Georgian Bay on one side and Lake Huron on the other, to Tobermory at the tip — about 310 km and 4 hours of driving. This segment ends at the Chi-Cheemaun ferry terminal; the crossing to Manitoulin itself is Segment 1b.
⚠️ Hwy 6 construction. A resurfacing project in Northern Bruce is causing delays on Hwy 6 through the 2026 season. Build a real buffer into the drive to Tobermory — Segment 1b’s one-hour ferry check-in cutoff is unforgiving.
⚠️ Bruce Peninsula National Park permits. Grotto / Cyprus Lake parking requires a confirmed reservation June 19 – September 7, in four-hour slots, tied to a specific vehicle and non-transferable. Outside those dates it’s first-come with self-registration. Campers do not get parking included.

★ MS Chi-Cheemaun Ferry — Tobermory Terminal
Vehicle ferry across Georgian Bay, Tobermory to South Baymouth. 1 hr 45 min on-peak, 2 hrs off-peak. Reservation required and check-in closes 1 hr before sailing. Propane and fuel must be declared.

Tobermory Harbourfront / Little Tub Harbour
The working harbour at the heart of town — docks, dive charters, and boat tour departures. An easy, flat waterfront stroll, good for an evening with a stroller.
ADDR Little Tub Harbour, Tobermory, ON N0H 2R0
GPS 45.2544, -81.6656
I1a-07
Fathom Five National Marine Park Visitor Centre
Parks Canada exhibits on the park’s 22 shipwrecks and geology, plus a lookout tower reached by a short climb. A land-based alternative to the Flowerpot Island boat trip.
ADDR 120 Chi Sin Tib Dek Rd, Tobermory, ON N0H 2R0
GPS 45.2531, -81.6647
I1a-08
Bluewater Park & Wiarton Willie Statue
Waterfront park on Colpoy’s Bay with the 7-tonne limestone statue of Wiarton Willie, Canada’s weather-forecasting groundhog. Quick, fun roadside stop on Hwy 6 heading up the peninsula.
ADDR 1125A Grey Rd 1, Wiarton, ON N0H 2T0
GPS 44.7442, -81.1367
