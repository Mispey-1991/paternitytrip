Northern Ontario Paternity Trip · Segment 1a of 13

Whitby → Tobermory

Hwy 10 N / Hwy 6 N via Owen Sound | ~310 km | ~4 hrs | 15 stops

The trip opens to the northwest rather than due north. Hwy 10 N and Hwy 6 N run up the narrowing limestone spine of the Bruce Peninsula, Georgian Bay on one side and Lake Huron on the other, to Tobermory at the tip — about 310 km and 4 hours of driving. This segment ends at the Chi-Cheemaun ferry terminal; the crossing to Manitoulin itself is Segment 1b.

Hwy 6 construction. A resurfacing project in Northern Bruce is causing delays on Hwy 6 through the 2026 season. Build a real buffer into the drive to Tobermory — Segment 1b's one-hour ferry check-in cutoff is unforgiving.
Bruce Peninsula National Park permits. Grotto / Cyprus Lake parking requires a confirmed reservation June 19 – September 7, in four-hour slots, tied to a specific vehicle and non-transferable. Outside those dates it's first-come with self-registration. Campers do not get parking included.
