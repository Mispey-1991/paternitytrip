Northern Ontario Paternity Trip · Segment 5 of 13

Marathon → Thunder Bay

Hwy 17 then Hwy 11/17 | ~3.5 hrs | ~330 km | 7 stops
The Lake Superior shoreline continues to deliver dramatic scenery all the way to Thunder Bay. Two of the most iconic stops in Northern Ontario — the Terry Fox Memorial and Ouimet Canyon — are on this leg.
📵 📵 No cell coverage between Marathon and Terrace Bay, or between Nipigon and Schreiber.
⛽ ⛽ Fuel at Terrace Bay, Schreiber and Nipigon — the best-spaced stretch of the Superior coast.
⚠️ 🏥 Hospitals at Marathon (Wilson Memorial), Terrace Bay (McCausland, 2 Cartier Dr) and Nipigon (Nipigon District Memorial). Manitouwadge General is a 54 km detour off the highway.

Points of Interest

I5-01
★ Eagle Canyon Suspension Bridge (Ouimet Canyon)
Canada's longest pedestrian suspension bridge (600 ft long, 152 ft above the canyon floor), plus a shorter second bridge over Eagle Canyon and Bat Lake. Short ~2.3 km loop trail down into the canyon. Just off Ouimet Canyon Road, ~45 min from Thunder Bay — easy to pair with Ouimet Canyon same day.
ADDR 275 Valley Road, Dorion, ON
GPS 48.7947113, -88.6137117
LINK https://www.eaglecanyonadventures.ca/
Researched by Mike
I5-02
Mount McKay & Hillcrest Park
183m rise above Thunder Bay; extraordinary views of the Sleeping Giant.
ADDR Mount McKay, Thunder Bay District, ON P7J 1J1, Canada
GPS 48.345278, -89.285556
I5-03
Nipigon River Bridge
Lake Superior's largest tributary; site of the 1915 world-record 14.5lb brook trout catch.
ADDR 403 ON-11, Nipigon, ON P0T 0A4, Canada
GPS 49.0197723, -88.2506672
I5-05
Terry Fox Memorial & Scenic Lookout
East of Thunder Bay; marks where Terry Fox's Marathon of Hope ended, Sept 1 1980 (143 days, 5,373km).
ADDR 1000 ON-11 #17, Thunder Bay, ON P7A 0A1, Canada
GPS 48.48497, -89.1679229
