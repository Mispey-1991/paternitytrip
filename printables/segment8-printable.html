Northern Ontario Paternity Trip · Segment 8 of 13

Hearst → Cochrane

Hwy 11 E/S | ~2.5 hrs | ~215 km | 5 stops
The highway bends south through Kapuskasing and into Cochrane — a town that sits on the Arctic Watershed and is the jumping-off point for James Bay.
⛽ ⛽ Fuel at Hearst, Kapuskasing, Smooth Rock Falls and Cochrane — reasonably spaced, but fill before dark; some stations close early.
🦌 🦌 Moose remain a frequent hazard along Hwy 11 at dawn and dusk.
⚠️ ⚠️ Check Ontario 511 before departing — Hwy 11 closures in this corridor often have no practical detour.
