Segment 3

Sault Ste. Marie → Wawa

Hwy 17 N · ~235 km · ~2.5 hrs

⚠️ Key Advisories

  • No cell service SSM–Wawa; fill up fuel in Sault Ste. Marie
  • Montreal River Hill grade — watch for trucks
  • Moose hazard dawn/dusk; reduce speed at night

Points of Interest

93 Mission Rd, Wawa, ON P0S 1K0
47.9735333, -84.7829472
8.5m steel Canada goose since 1960; 'Wawa' is Ojibwe for wild goose.

Hikes

H3-01 Agawa Rock Pictographs Trail
Algoma, Unorganized, North Part, ON
47.368013, -84.6991942
0.8km loop, 30–60 min, steep/rocky, wave-exposed. 35 ancient Ojibwe ochre images incl. Mishipeshu.
H3-02 Edmund Fitzgerald Lookout Trail
Pancake Bay Provincial Park, Algoma, ON P0S 1A0
46.9812348, -84.7001871
5km trail from Pancake Bay Provincial Park; the Fitzgerald sank within sight of this shoreline, Nov 1975.
H3-03 Nokomis Trail
Algoma, Unorganized, North Part, ON P0S 1K0
47.7919444, -84.8962224
5km loop from Old Woman Bay, 2–3 hrs. Ranked among Canada's top 5 day hikes.
H3-04 Old Woman Bay ★
Old Woman Bay, Algoma, ON P0S 1K0
47.7853504, -84.9105310
Roadside pull-off, short walk to the water. One of the most photographed Lake Superior views; minimal effort.
H3-05 Sand River (Pinguisibi) Trail
Algoma, Unorganized, North Part, ON P0S 1A0
47.4328634, -84.7307571
6km return past a series of waterfalls, 1.5–3 hrs, easy-moderate.
