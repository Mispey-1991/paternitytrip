Northern Ontario Paternity Trip · Segment 6 of 13

Thunder Bay → Geraldton

Hwy 11 E | ~2.5 hrs | ~230 km | 13 stops
Leaving the Lake Superior coast behind, this segment heads into the boreal interior on Highway 11. Geraldton is a practical overnight with a fascinating gold mining past.
⛽ Fill at Nipigon before turning north on Hwy 11. Beardmore and Jellicoe are very small; Geraldton is the next dependable fuel.
🦌 Moose are a frequent hazard on Hwy 11, worst at dawn and dusk.
⚠️ Check Ontario 511 before departing — when Hwy 11 closes in this corridor there is often no practical detour.
⚠️ Nearest emergency care: Geraldton District Hospital.
